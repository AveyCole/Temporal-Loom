How this instrument works

Free drift slowly moves through fragments. Audio mode measures bass energy and transients; stronger impulses advance the image pair. MIDI notes choose images by note number, CC1 scrubs the transition, and CC74 sets distortion.

Morphing combines crossfades, blend modes, and displaced scanline slices. It is a real-time collage, not an AI semantic interpolation.

Exact-day searches may return nothing. Increase the radius explicitly; dates are never silently widened. Archive services may be slow, unavailable, or restrict some captures.
